Questions, answered

What owners ask before they sign up.

Anything else, ask Caleb on your call, or email caleb@getworkbuddy.com.

Who are you? Is this legit? Where are you based?

We’re WorkBuddy. Caleb Benedict founded it, and we’re based in Massachusetts.

We’re not Google, and we don’t pretend to be. Nobody can sell you a spot in Google’s free results, not Google and not anybody else.

What we do is the work that earns you the spot: your Google page, your website, your reviews, and content about your town and your services. Search us on Google, and ask Caleb anything you want on a call.

How much does it cost, and what’s included?

$297 a month covers all of it, done for you:

  • Your Google page, actively managed. Fully built out, new posts every week, and every review answered within a day.
  • Your website, rebuilt to rank. Fast, mobile-ready, with a page for every service you offer and hosting included.
  • Local SEO. Keyword research, every page optimized, and a new article every week.
  • Reviews on autopilot. Review requests to the customers you share with us.
  • Calls and leads. An instant text reply to website leads, a business number and an assistant for missed calls.
  • AI search and listings. Set up so ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI can find and recommend you, plus accurate listings on the major directories.
  • A dedicated account manager you can text, and a dashboard to see your results.

No contract, and no setup fee. Cancel any month.

Is there a contract? What if I want to cancel?

No contract. It’s $297 a month and you can cancel any month.

What stays yours. Your domain is registered in your name. You’re the owner of your Google Business Profile; we’re only added as a manager. Your phone number and your reviews are yours. The website is yours from day one. If you leave, at any time, we move it wherever you want at no cost.

What stops when you stop paying is the work: the posts on your Google page, the new pages and articles, the ranking tracking and the monthly report. That’s a service, not something you own.

What do you need from me, and how much work is it for me?

Very little. It’s done for you. We rebuild the website, write the weekly articles, post on your Google page, and answer your reviews.

That’s the whole point: you’re out running jobs, and this keeps working while you are.

What we do need from you to get started is a bit of your time up front. There’s a fair amount to set up, but we walk you through every step. Most owners are fully onboarded within a day to a week, depending on their pace.

How long until I see results, or until it’s live?

Live: most owners are fully onboarded within a day to a week, depending on their pace. We walk you through every step.

Results: rankings build week by week. Some of our clients moved up within two or three weeks; how fast depends on your town, your trade and your competition. Every new article and every new review keeps building on it.

I’ve been burned before. How are you different?

Fair question. Most of the owners we talk to have been burned too. Three things are different here.

No contract. It’s $297 a month and you can cancel any month, so we have to earn it every month.

We don’t sell you leads. We get your own business found on Google Search, Google Maps, and AI search, so the calls come straight to you, not to a list you share with five other contractors.

What’s yours stays yours. Your website is yours from day one, your domain is in your name, you own your Google Business Profile, and your phone number and reviews are yours. If it’s not working, you leave and keep all of it.

Will this actually make my phone ring, or just get me rankings?

Rankings nobody calls from are worthless to you, so here’s the honest answer.

Almost nobody scrolls past the first page. When someone in your town needs what you do, they call one of the first few names they see, usually from the map at the top. That’s where we put you, and the website is built to turn the people who find you into calls.

Most of my work comes from referrals. Why do I need this?

Referrals are great. Keep them. But most people who hear your name still look you up before they call.

If they search and find a thin Google page, old reviews, or the guy down the street first, some of them call him instead. Being findable protects the referrals you already earned, and it brings in the people who don’t have anybody to ask.

The guy down the street has hundreds of reviews. Can I even catch him?

Reviews matter, but they’re not the only thing Google looks at. It also looks at how complete and active your Google page is, how well your website matches what people search for, and how close you are to the person searching.

That’s the stuff most owners never touch, and it’s what we work on every week. On the call, Caleb will show you exactly who’s above you and why, so you can see the gap for yourself.

I tried SEO before and it didn’t work.

A lot of owners have. The old SEO pitch tried to rank a website anywhere in the country. What we do is local: your Google page, the map results, your reviews, and pages written about your town and your services, plus showing up when people ask ChatGPT or Google’s AI who to hire.

And there’s no contract this time, so if it’s not working, you leave.

Can’t I just do this myself?

Honestly? Yes, if you have the time. Here’s the list:

  • Build out and post to your Google page every week
  • Answer every review within a day
  • Ask every customer for a review
  • Write a new local article every week
  • Build and keep up a page for every service
  • Keep your listings the same on every directory
  • Set up for AI search, and keep checking it
  • Track where you rank, every week

Some owners do it at night after the jobs are done. If you’d rather spend your nights on something else, that’s what the $297 is for.

Once you’re on board, you have a dedicated account manager, and you can text us any time: a change to your website, a question about your Google page, anything.

You also get a login to your dashboard, where you can edit your site, check your reviews and manage billing.
